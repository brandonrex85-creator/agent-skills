## Why this exists (secondary block)

Most engineering advice is one person's experience generalized into a rule.

We're doing something narrower and more checkable: taking a large body of documented
practice, extracting the specific failure modes practitioners name, and reporting what
shows up more than once.

Sometimes the answer is boring. Sometimes 15 of 24 independent disciplines turn out to
name the exact same enemy, and that's worth an issue.

---

## What you get (three-up)

**Patterns, not takes**
Findings come from counting across disciplines. If we can't show the work, we don't
run it.

**Specific enough to use**
No "write better tests." The actual excuse, the actual correction, the thing to do
this week.

**Short**
Under 1,000 words. One idea per issue.

## Notes for whoever builds this

- The headline leads with the finding, not the publication. Nobody cares about a new
  newsletter; they care that their deferral backlog is untracked.
- The reassurance line under the button matters more than it looks — "one email a week"
  is the objection being pre-answered.
- Do not add a countdown, a fake subscriber number, or "join 1,000+ engineers." There
  are zero subscribers. Say nothing rather than something false.
- Issue 001 should be readable *without* subscribing. Gating the only proof of quality
  behind the conversion is how first-time visitors bounce.
