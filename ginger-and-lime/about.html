Our story

Health food that tastes like a treat, not a rule

We opened Ginger & Lime because eating well around here usually meant choosing between something good for you and something you actually wanted to eat. We did not think that was a fair trade.

How we cook

Prepped in-house, from the hummus up

The pico de gallo, the hummus, the pickled cabbage, the slaw, the bread — we make them here. The prime rib is cooked low and slow in our own kitchen and sliced thin to order. The juice is squeezed when you ask for it, never poured from a carton.

That is slower than the alternative, and it is the whole point. Fresh produce does not need much help; it just needs someone willing to do the work.

What we care about

Three things we will not cut corners on

Produce first

Vegetables and fruit are the base of nearly everything on our menu — not a garnish added at the end.

Made here, daily

Sauces, spreads, pickles and bread are made in our kitchen so we know exactly what is in them.

Room for everyone

Vegan, gluten-free, or a full prime rib sandwich — there is something on the board for whoever you walked in with.

Come say hi

You will find us in the Seabreeze Shopping Center

We are open every day from 10:30 in the morning until 7:00 at night. Walk in, call ahead, or order pickup — whatever fits your day.
