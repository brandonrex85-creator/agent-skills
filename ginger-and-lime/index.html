Our story

A neighborhood kitchen with a health-food heart

Ginger & Lime started with a simple idea: eating well should taste like something you actually look forward to. We cook the way we would at home — slower, simpler, and with produce we would happily eat raw.

You will find us in the Seabreeze Shopping Center, squeezing carrots and ginger behind the counter from open to close.
